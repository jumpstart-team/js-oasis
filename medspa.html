Services

Every treatment starts with a consultation, so the plan is built around your skin and your goals.

Skincare and medical-grade peels

Customized treatments to nourish, repair, and protect your skin: clear acne, soften the appearance of fine lines, or restore your natural glow, using PCA Skincare and L'Bri products.

Microneedling

A minimally invasive treatment using tiny medical-grade needles to stimulate the skin's natural healing process and support collagen production, which can improve texture and tone and soften the appearance of fine lines, acne scars, and enlarged pores. The Collagen P.I.N.® is FDA cleared to improve the appearance of facial acne scars in adults 22 and older, Fitzpatrick Skin Types I to III.

Anti-wrinkle injections (Xeomin)

Subtle, strategic treatment that softens the appearance of lines without changing your natural expression. Administered by trained injectors who tailor each treatment to your facial anatomy. Xeomin is also used clinically to relax targeted muscles in tension headaches and TMJ pain.

IV hydration

Fluids, vitamins, and nutrients to support hydration, energy, and recovery. Often chosen around travel, stress, or a simple wellness reset. Base IV fluid is $199, plus $29 per medication additive.

Light therapy (LightStim)

A gentle, non-invasive LED treatment that supports skin healing, helps calm inflammation and acne, and supports collagen production. Works on its own or paired with other skincare services.
