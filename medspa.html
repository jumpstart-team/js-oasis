Services

Skincare & medical-grade peels

Customized treatments to nourish, repair, and protect your skin: clear acne, reduce fine lines and wrinkles, or restore your natural glow with PCA Skincare and L'Bri products tailored to your skin's needs.

Microneedling

A minimally invasive treatment using tiny, medical-grade needles to stimulate your skin's natural healing process and boost collagen production, improving texture, tone, and reducing the appearance of fine lines, acne scars, and enlarged pores. The Collagen P.I.N.® is FDA cleared to improve the appearance of facial acne scars in adults 22 and older, Fitzpatrick Skin Types I–III.

Anti-wrinkle injections (Xeomin)

Subtle, strategic enhancements that refresh your look without changing your natural expression. Administered by skilled injectors who tailor each treatment to your facial anatomy. More on Xeomin below.

IV hydration

Essential fluids, vitamins, and nutrients to support immunity, energy, and recovery. Ideal for travel, stress, or a simple wellness reset. See the full menu below.

Light therapy (LightStim)

A gentle, non-invasive LED treatment that supports skin healing, reduces inflammation and acne, and stimulates collagen production. Works on its own or paired with other skincare services.

Why choose Xeomin?

Xeomin is one of the most effective and minimally invasive treatments for reducing fine lines and wrinkles (crow's feet, forehead lines, frown lines) without surgery. Our expert injectors tailor each treatment to your unique facial anatomy for natural-looking results. Xeomin can also treat tension headaches and TMJ pain by relaxing targeted muscles, with the added benefit of looking younger.

  • Smooths and softens wrinkles
  • Quick, in-office treatment with no downtime
  • Subtle, long-lasting results
  • Safe and FDA-approved
