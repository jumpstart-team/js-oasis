Services

Urgent care

Our providers are ER-trained with years of experience in acute injuries and illness for patients of all ages: coughs, strep, mono, pneumonia, fractures and sprains, lacerations, infections, STIs and contraception care, and school physicals. Labs and full bloodwork are available onsite; X-rays are available at a partner site with results in 1–2 hours.

Primary care

Convenient access to primary care with attention to your unique health needs. Our providers listen to your treatment preferences and work with you to build a care plan that promotes wellness while actively managing disease.

Prescription weight loss

Medical support, counseling, and GLP therapies, with additional curated medications as needed. GLP medications increase fullness hormones and suppress the drive to overeat — used under clinical supervision, most patients see 10–20% weight loss with weekly-to-monthly visits to adjust the plan.

DOT exams

No need to wait weeks for an appointment — update your DOT physical requirement here so you can stay on the road.

Pre-employment physicals

In-clinic pre-employment physicals and drug and alcohol screening (non-DOT) for your employees, usually with same-day appointments available.
