What we treat

Our providers are emergency-trained, with years of experience in acute injury and illness for patients of every age.

Urgent care

Walk-ins welcome

Coughs, strep, mono, pneumonia, fractures and sprains, lacerations, infections, STI and contraception care, and school physicals. Labs and full bloodwork are available onsite. X-rays are available at a partner site with results in 1 to 2 hours.

Primary care and disease management

Ongoing care

Convenient access to primary care with attention to your health needs. Our providers listen to your treatment preferences and build a care plan with you that promotes wellness while actively managing disease.

DOT and pre-employment physicals

Usually same-day

No need to wait weeks for an appointment. Update your DOT physical here so you can stay on the road. We also handle in-clinic pre-employment physicals and non-DOT drug and alcohol screening for your employees.
