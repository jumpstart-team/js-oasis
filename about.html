The people behind Oasis

We are owned by clinicians, and our vision is to make healthcare accessible and affordable. Healthcare is scary for a lot of people, so we work to remove the barriers.

Since 2020

Oasis has served this community since 2020, caring for patients across the lifespan, from children to adults.

We offer several ways in, from commercial insurance to flat cash rates to membership, so that people can get care whatever their situation. That is the whole idea: meet patients where they are.

Providers

The clinicians you will see at Oasis.

Laura Mckinnis, APNP

Owner and Provider

With 15 years of ER experience, Laura offers expertise in acute illness and injury. After years of hospital-based emergency medicine, she chose to bring clinical expertise and convenience directly to patients. Children's and women's health are a particular joy. Oasis has provided accessible care since 2020, offering insurance, flat rates, and membership so that healthcare is available regardless of income.

Katie Henke, NP

Specialty Care Provider

Katie is an experienced nurse practitioner specializing in the diagnosis and management of Postural Orthostatic Tachycardia Syndrome (POTS), related autonomic disorders, and headache disorders. She also offers primary care for adolescents and adults. Specialized care for these chronic neurologic disorders can be hard to find, so Katie built her practice around evidence-based treatment and care plans built around each patient.
