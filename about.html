The people behind Oasis.

We're owned by clinicians, and our vision is to make healthcare accessible and affordable. Healthcare is scary for a lot of people, so we work to remove the barriers: multiple ways to access our clinic, from commercial insurance to membership, across everyday care, specialty care, and aesthetics. Since 2020, our team has provided accessible healthcare and wellness services to the Grafton community, for patients across the lifespan from children to adults.

Providers

L
Laura Mckinnis, APNP
Owner & Provider

With 15 years of ER experience, Laura offers expertise in acute illness and injury. After years of hospital-based emergency medicine, she chose to bring clinical expertise and convenience directly to patients. Children's and women's health are a particular joy. Oasis has provided accessible care since 2020, offering insurance, flat rates, and clinic subscription plans so healthcare is available to everyone, regardless of income.

K
Katie Henke, NP
Specialty Care Provider

Katie is an experienced nurse practitioner specializing in the diagnosis and management of Postural Orthostatic Tachycardia Syndrome (POTS) and related autonomic disorders, and headache disorders. She also offers primary care for adolescents and adults. Recognizing the limited access many patients face when seeking specialized care for these chronic neurologic disorders, Katie is committed to advancing patient outcomes through evidence-based treatment and individualized care plans.

She finds it a joy to problem-solve with and listen to her patients to help address their healthcare needs and concerns. Languages spoken: English, Spanish.
