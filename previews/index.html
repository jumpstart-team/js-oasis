Three homepage directions

Three genuinely different visual directions for the Oasis Healthcare homepage, each built off a site Laura named in the intake form as inspiration. Adjusted per her own notes on what each one was missing. Same real content and facts throughout (pricing, providers, hours); what changes is color, type, and structure. These are drafts for comparison, not live pages, and none of the rest of the site (healthcare/membership/medspa/about/contact) has been restyled to match any of them yet. That happens after one direction is picked.

Chosen direction

Option C, now the live site

Laura and Angela picked Option C, so it stopped being a preview: the whole site is now built in it. Hero on “from illness to wellness,” the four care categories, then the ways to pay for care. Lighter and brighter than the original C (the dusty tan is gone), shorter copy throughout, aimed between Navēa and Ethos.

All six pages share one stylesheet now, so there is no separate v2 preview file to drift out of date. The three originals below stay frozen for reference only.

The three original options

A: Energetic Modern

Inspired by: MDRN Wellness Medical Spa
Her note: “modern look, movement and imagery,” but “needs to be more medicine based.”

Bold geometric type (Outfit), a dense service grid, confident scale. Credentials and evidence-based language pushed to the front to add clinical weight the reference was missing.

View option A

B: Calm & Integrative

Inspired by: Navēa Wellness
Her note: “very calm, peaceful... good for medspa but needs more medicine.”

A soft serif (Lora) with a rounded body sans, generous whitespace, minimal color. A provider quote and credentials section added deliberately, so it doesn't read as spa-only.

View option B

C: Trusted DPC

Inspired by: Ethos Modern Medicine
Her note: “they're doing what we're doing,” but “depth is a little weak and pictures need improvement.”

A trust-forward serif (Source Serif) on navy and cream, real membership pricing shown right on the homepage, a numbered differentiation section. Depth built through layout and elevation, not photography.

View option C
