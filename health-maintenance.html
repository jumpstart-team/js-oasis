Health maintenance and prevention.

Medically supervised weight loss, annual physicals, and preventive screening. Care aimed at keeping you well, not just treating you when you are unwell.

Book an appointment About weight loss support

Weight loss support

Medical support, counseling, and GLP therapies, with additional curated medications as needed. GLP medications increase fullness hormones and reduce the drive to overeat.

Under clinical supervision, most patients see 10 to 20% weight loss, with weekly-to-monthly visits to adjust the plan. A large share of our patients find Oasis through this program.
