What is Direct Primary Care?

Direct Primary Care (DPC) is an innovative healthcare model that offers personalized, affordable, and accessible primary care through a simple, fee-based plan. Members get extended visits, direct access to their provider, and a focus on preventative care, all without insurance middlemen.

Who this is for

  • Small business owners and their families
  • Self-employed patients without employer coverage
  • Anyone between insurance plans
  • Patients who want more time with their provider than a typical visit allows

Membership vs. a typical insurance-based visit

Oasis Healthcare membership

  • Extended visit time to actually talk through your concerns
  • Direct messaging with your provider between visits
  • One flat monthly fee: no surprise bills
  • No insurance approval needed before you're seen

Typical insurance-based visit

  • Short visit windows driven by insurer scheduling
  • Referrals and prior authorization often required
  • Costs vary by plan, deductible, and coverage
  • Access depends on network and plan rules
