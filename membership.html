What Direct Primary Care means

Direct Primary Care, or DPC, is a membership model. You pay the clinic a flat monthly fee directly, and that fee covers your primary care rather than being billed per visit.

What it buys is time and access: longer appointments, direct messaging with your provider between visits, and a focus on prevention. For some patients that is a better fit than a per-visit model. For others, insurance is the right route, and we take that too.

Membership compared with a per-visit model

A plain description of the difference, so you can judge which suits you.

With membership

  • Longer appointments, built for conversation
  • Direct messaging with your provider between visits
  • One flat monthly fee, known in advance
  • No per-visit billing for primary and urgent care services

With insurance or cash

  • Standard visit lengths
  • Costs vary by plan, deductible, and coverage
  • Cash-pay visits run $149 to $349
  • Most commercial insurances accepted for everyday visits
